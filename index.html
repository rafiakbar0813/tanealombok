philosophy

a place to
feel at ease

Inspired by the word “tana,” meaning earth and a place to stay, tanéa guesthouse is nestled in Mertak Village, Central Lombok, offering a peaceful sanctuary for travelers seeking warmth, tranquility, and balance amidst nature.

────── a gentle way to stay
────── Feel safe, feel free

made for her

Tanéa is a female-friendly guesthouse created with comfort, privacy, and peace of mind. Whether travelling solo or simply looking for a quieter escape, every stay is designed to feel safe, welcoming, and effortless.

work, rest, reset

While work carries on, Tanéa offers a peaceful space to work in comfort, take a moment to rest, and return with a clearer mind. A stay designed for workcations, where productivity meets relaxation.

────── Work at your own pace

discover your experience

Indulge in moments of deep relaxation, discover the beauty of Lombok through thoughtfully curated journeys, and embrace exciting adventures surrounded by the island’s breathtaking landscapes. From soothing spa treatments to cultural discoveries and outdoor explorations, each experience is designed to enrich your stay, create lasting memories, and connect you with the natural charm and authentic spirit of Lombok.
